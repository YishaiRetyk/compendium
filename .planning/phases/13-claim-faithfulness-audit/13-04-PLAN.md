Add a "### Page-marker convention" subsection to AGENTS.md §6 immediately AFTER the "### Locator Types" table. Document, with ABSTRACT placeholders only:

- Markdown-native raw sources lose page boundaries, so `#p<n>` locators have nothing to bound against. The OPTIONAL `<!-- page: N -->` HTML-comment marker, hand-inserted in the raw source at each page break, makes `#p` resolvable where authors opt in. It is Obsidian-invisible (does not render), grep-able, and requires NO change to the `[prov:]` grammar (`#p` already exists in the Locator Types table).
- Slice semantics: `#p8` resolves from the `<!-- page: 8 -->` marker to the line before `<!-- page: 9 -->`; `#p12-14` spans the `<!-- page: 12 -->` marker to the `<!-- page: 15 -->` marker (exclusive upper bound).
- Optional, with a documented fallback: markers are never required. When present, `#p` resolves to a bounded passage; when absent, a `#p` locator degrades to the audit's first-class `insufficient-locator` verdict (NOT an error) — this nudges authors toward `#sec:`/`#para` locators. Unmarked paginated sources are not errors (D-06).
- Document-now / helper-later (D-07): the curator hand-marks sources today; any auto-insertion helper (e.g. PDF→md page-break detection at ingest) is deferred to a later version. The audit consumes the markers read-only; ingest gains zero new logic.

Use placeholders such as `<source-id>`, `<YYYY-MM-DD-slug>`, and literal `N` in any example. Do NOT use any real vault slug, page title, or term (CLAUDE.md §3 neutrality rule — template-public file).
