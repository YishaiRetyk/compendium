## Task 1 — RED test harness

`tests/phase-09/test_lint_mask_fences.sh` (phase-09 dir per the 260602-d6a precedent —
that is where the lint masking test family lives). Self-contained temp wiki, neutral
fixtures. Cases:

- T1 (WR-02): unclosed fence → zero findings
- T2 (WR-03): ```ruby pseudo-closer → does not close, zero findings to EOF
- T3: pseudo-closer then TRUE closer → masked inside, bare link AFTER flagged (anti-over-masking)
- T4: normal closed fence with trailing-whitespace closer (regression)
- T5: closer must be >= opener length (4-tick opener not closed by 3-tick line)
- T6: closer char must match (~~~ does not close ```)
- T7: --fix leaves unclosed/pseudo-closed files byte-identical (mutation guard)

## Task 2 — Implementation

Replace the paired-only `_FENCE_RE` regex in `mask_markdown` with a line-based
`_mask_fences` scanner implementing the CommonMark rules above. Length-preserving
(each fence-block line blanked to same-length spaces; newlines kept) so positional
`--fix` splicing stays correct. Column-0 anchoring retained from the prior regex
(no 0–3-space indent tolerance) to keep the diff behavior-minimal.
