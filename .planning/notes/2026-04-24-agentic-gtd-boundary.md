## Core Boundary

The compendium should be the durable memory and review layer in a broader
agentic GTD system. It should not become the task backend.

## Three-Layer Model

- Task layer: executable commitments, next actions, reminders, calendar,
  waiting-for mechanics, and transactional state.
- Working-memory layer: recent conversations, scratch context, inbox material,
  and short-lived reasoning state.
- Wiki-compiler layer: durable synthesis, project support material, decisions,
  long-term preferences, provenance-backed beliefs, and reflective memory.

## Good Fits For The Wiki Compiler

- Reference and project support material.
- Decisions and rationale.
- Patterns across notes, journals, reading, and conversations.
- Higher-horizon thinking and long-term preferences.
- Rich someday/maybe ideas once they become conceptually substantial.
- Review support that surfaces stale areas, contradictions, neglected projects,
  and durable insights.

## Poor Fits For The Wiki Compiler

- Inbox capture as a primary interface.
- Next-action execution.
- Calendar and reminders.
- Rapid transactional updates.
- High-churn waiting-for state.
- Slack, ticket, invoice, order, or event-stream systems of record.

## Roadmap Handling

- Use Phase 12.1 to document the boundary and routing rules.
- Keep observed GTD review patterns in backlog until real usage produces stable
  recurring views.
- Avoid adding GTD-specific page types, filesystem taxonomies, or canonical
  dashboards before observed practice justifies them.
