# Dario Amodei

## TL;DR

Dario Amodei is co-founder and CEO of [[anthropic|Anthropic]]. In a 2026 on-stage debate with [[demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], he took the faster side on [[agi-timelines|AGI timelines]] — expecting a model to do most or all of what software engineers do "end to end" within six to twelve months, and the [[ai-self-improvement-loop|self-improvement loop]] of models good at coding and AI research to drive acceleration [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:01:12-00:02:44|direct|2026-06-14]. He reiterated his central policy recommendation: not selling advanced chips to geopolitical adversaries [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:22:16-00:23:30|direct|2026-06-14].

## Key Facts

- Expects a model to do most or all of what software engineers do end-to-end within six to twelve months [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:01:12-00:02:44|direct|2026-06-14] [epistemic:: tentative]
- States Anthropic's revenue grew 10x over three years — roughly 100 million (2023), 1 billion (2024), 10 billion (2025) [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:06:08-00:07:39|direct|2026-06-14] [epistemic:: tentative]
- Reaffirms a one-to-five-year horizon for significant entry-level white-collar job displacement, while acknowledging the labor market's historical adaptability [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:15:44-00:17:30|direct|2026-06-14] [epistemic:: tentative]
- Traces Anthropic's safety lineage to mechanistic interpretability — "looking inside the model's brain" — and positions himself as risk-aware but anti-doomer [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:25:21-00:26:44|direct|2026-06-14]
- Names not selling advanced chips to adversaries as the single biggest lever to buy time on safety [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:22:16-00:23:30|direct|2026-06-14] [epistemic:: tentative]

## Detail

Amodei's framing of the [[ai-self-improvement-loop|self-improvement loop]] is mechanistic: models that are good at coding and AI research produce the next generation and speed up development, with code and research as the key accelerants. He cautions that not every part of the loop can be sped up by AI — chip manufacture and model training time remain physical bottlenecks [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:01:12-00:02:44|direct|2026-06-14].

On Anthropic's commercial trajectory he describes an exponential relationship between a model's cognitive capability and the revenue it generates, citing the 10x-over-three-years growth path [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:06:08-00:07:39|direct|2026-06-14]. He frames both Anthropic and Google as research-led companies with hard scientific problems as a north star.

On safety, he frames his forthcoming risk essay around a scene from Carl Sagan's *Contact* — "how did you get through this technological adolescence without destroying yourselves?" — and names bioterrorism, authoritarian misuse, and labor displacement as concrete worries [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:09:44-00:13:16|direct|2026-06-14]. His safety work at Anthropic traces to mechanistic interpretability, and he is explicitly skeptical of doomerism while treating misalignment as a real, addressable risk [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:25:21-00:26:44|direct|2026-06-14].

On geopolitics, his recommendation is unchanged: not selling advanced chips to adversaries, arguing an enforceable mutual slowdown is infeasible while adversaries build at a similar pace. He rejects the "bind China into US supply chains" rationale, comparing chip sales to selling nuclear weapons to North Korea for a manufacturer's profit [prov:src-2026-06-14-hassabis-amodei-day-after-agi#t00:23:40-00:24:48|direct|2026-06-14].
