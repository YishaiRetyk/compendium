# StandardAgents

## TL;DR

StandardAgents is a stealth-mode startup co-founded by [[justin-schroeder|Justin Schroeder]], building an ecosystem of [[domain-specific-agents|Domain-Specific Agents]] — small, isolated, composable agents coordinated under a top-level agent, communicating in plain English. As of the mid-2026 talk it had not announced a product but was using the architecture internally on a daily basis and offering early access at standardagents.ai. Its public claims (the source of the concept's empirical figures — >80% token efficiency per task, a "137× cheaper" small-model comparison, and a 2026 token-cost-rise tracker) all originate from Schroeder's talk and are unverified internal measurements.

## Detail

StandardAgents is the vehicle through which [[justin-schroeder|Justin Schroeder]] is commercializing the [[domain-specific-agents|Domain-Specific Agents]] architecture. Because the company is pre-announcement, essentially everything on this page derives from a single conference talk and should be read as the founder's framing rather than independently verified fact — hence the page-level `tentative` grading. Its distinctive public artifacts are (1) the architectural argument itself, (2) a set of internal efficiency figures used to motivate it, and (3) a token-cost tracker used to argue that rising inference prices make cheap, narrow agents more valuable over time. Schroeder positions the company at the front of a trend he predicts will accelerate through late 2026 into a 2027 "year of multi-agent orchestration."
