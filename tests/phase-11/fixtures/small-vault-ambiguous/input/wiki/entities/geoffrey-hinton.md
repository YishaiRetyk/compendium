# Geoffrey Hinton

## TL;DR

- Computer scientist known as a pioneer of deep learning.
- Professor emeritus at the University of Toronto.

## Key Facts

- Awarded the 2018 Turing Award with Bengio and LeCun.
- Co-authored the backpropagation paper in 1986.

## Detail

Hinton has been central to the modern deep learning revolution.
