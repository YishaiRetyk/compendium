# Transformer

## TL;DR

- Transformers are attention-based sequence models. [epistemic:: inferred]
- They replaced recurrent networks for most language tasks. [epistemic:: inferred]

## Key Facts

- Introduced by Vaswani et al. in 2017. [epistemic:: inferred]
- Scale roughly with compute and data. [epistemic:: inferred]

## Detail

The transformer architecture stacks self-attention and feed-forward
layers.  See [[Attention Mechanism]].
